## Slide 2 — What does the robot do, and what does it not do?

### 建议图示

```text
Pack positioned and identified
→ PLC: safe-to-connect
→ Robot: locate / grasp / align / insert / confirm
→ PLC permits test
→ Tester performs electrical and functional EoL test
→ Tester/PLC: de-energized and safe-to-disconnect
→ Robot: unlock / extract / return harness
→ MES associates result and releases or routes the pack
```

### 系统责任

| System | Responsibility in the reference abstraction |
| --- | --- |
| Robot / connector automation | Physical connector and harness manipulation |
| Test equipment | Electrical and functional test execution |
| PLC / safety system | Permission, interlocks, safe state and safe stop |
| MES / traceability | Pack identity, recipe and test-result association |
| Fixture / station | Pack positioning and station support |

### 口头核心句

> The robot does not perform the electrical test. It establishes and removes the physical connection under PLC and safety permission. This distinction prevents the robot task, the test process and the safety logic from being mixed together.

### 限定

这是一套由工序基线、设备资料、企业披露和工程推理形成的`reference abstraction`，不是CATL现场逐帧复原。

## Slide 3 — Which requirements change the robotic task?

### 页面建议：把六类要求压缩为三组

```text
Physical manipulation
R1 Flexible-harness handling
R2 Contact-rich insertion

Process and safety
R3 Electrical/HV safety interface
R5 Connection-quality confirmation

Production and system integration
R4 Variation and changeover
R6 Traceability and integration
```

页面底部加一条逻辑：

```text
Task step → Why it matters → Effect on robot task → Required capability → Unknown
```

### 讲解时使用的完整六类要求

| ID | Requirement | Effect on the robot task | Example required capability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Flexible-harness handling | Connector pose changes with gravity, grasp point and motion history | DLO perception, adaptive grasp/support, tension management |
| R2 | Contact-rich insertion | Alignment alone does not prevent jamming or overload after contact | Force/torque sensing, compliance, search and recovery |
| R3 | Electrical/HV safety interface | The robot cannot decide insertion or removal timing independently | Safety I/O, PLC handshake, de-energization confirmation |
| R4 | Pack/connector variation | Fixed poses, tools and adapters may not cover changeovers | Identification, parameterized programs, tool/adapter strategy |
| R5 | Connection-quality confirmation | Physical insertion does not automatically prove locking or electrical connection | Configured force/position/vision/electrical confirmation |
| R6 | Traceability and integration | Actions and exceptions must remain associated with pack ID and test recipe | PLC/MES synchronization and operation records |

### 口头核心句

> The requirements were derived from the task steps, not from advertised humanoid capabilities. R1, R2, R5 and R6 also occur in other assembly domains, while R3 is the strongest battery-specific safety gate in this case.

### 口头指出两至四个代表性UNKNOWN即可

- 目标connector的类型、几何、锁止机构和力范围；
- CATL现场的safe-to-connect/disconnect状态机；
- 连接成功的实际判据组合；
- 独立验证的节拍、成功率、人工介入、成本和长期可靠性。

## Slide 4 — Conditional suitability, not architecture ranking

### 三种条件化结论

| Production scenario | Architecture worth prioritising | Mechanism and evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low variety, high volume, fixed interface and single station | Dedicated automation | Fixtures and mechanical guidance can absorb part of R1/R2 complexity; frequent hardware change may reduce its advantage |
| Moderate variation, stationary task and periodic changeover | Fixed industrial robot | Programmable motion, vision and force control can address R1/R2/R5; multi-station replication and brownfield integration may raise system cost |
| High variation, multiple stations and human-oriented brownfield layout | Mobile dual-arm/humanoid is worth evaluating | Mobility and two-arm support may be used across stations, but R3 safety integration, maturity, reliability and economics remain insufficiently evidenced |

### 口头核心句

> Architecture selection has two gates. Task requirements determine what the system must be able to do, while production conditions determine whether implementing those capabilities with a particular architecture is reasonable. A safety gate and an evidence gate can still block deployment or require the conclusion to be narrowed.

### 不应说

- “Humanoid is the most flexible and therefore the best.”
- “Dedicated automation is too rigid.”
- “There is no public evidence, so the architecture cannot work.”

### 应说

> Under high-variation, multi-station brownfield conditions, mobile dual-arm systems are worth evaluating; however, current evidence does not establish deployment readiness, economic superiority or the required battery-specific safety integration.

## 汇报前自检：能够不用讲义回答的五句话

1. **研究意图：**我不是从某种机器人出发寻找应用，而是从电池制造任务出发推导要求，再判断不同架构的条件适用性。
