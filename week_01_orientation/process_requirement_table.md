个体# Process Requirement Table

This table collects process-specific requirements that influence the suitability of robotic systems.

| Process Step | Material / Product | Key Requirements | Robotics Challenges | Possible Robotic Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mixing | Powders, solvent, slurry | Homogeneity, contamination control, recipe accuracy | Dust, chemical exposure, cleaning effort | Automated material feeding, container handling |
| Coating | Slurry and current collector foil | Uniform coating thickness, stable web handling | Continuous web process, high sensitivity to defects | Roll handling, sample handling, inspection support |
| Drying | Coated electrode foil | Controlled drying, solvent removal, defect prevention | High-temperature process, integrated line equipment | Limited direct role; support logistics and monitoring |
| Calendering | Dry coated electrode foil | Target thickness, density, porosity, surface quality | Roll-to-roll precision, risk of damaging electrode | Roll transport, changeover assistance |
| Slitting | Electrode roll | Clean cuts, low burr formation, particle control | Thin flexible material, sharp edges, particle generation | Roll loading, unloading, inspection support |
| Vacuum drying | Electrode rolls or sheets | Low residual moisture, dry environment | Dry room compatibility, thermal process integration | Automated loading/unloading |
| Notching / cutting | Electrode sheets or rolls | Geometry accuracy, low burrs, low particles | Thin flexible sheets, positioning accuracy | Handling, machine loading, visual inspection |
| Stacking / winding | Anode, cathode, separator | High alignment accuracy, stable tension, no damage | Delicate flexible materials, high speed, precision | Vision-guided handling, alignment support |
| Tab welding | Cell stack or jelly roll, tabs | Strong weld, low resistance, no thermal damage | Process quality monitoring, precise positioning | Part positioning, loading, inspection |
| Housing insertion | Cell core and housing/pouch | No deformation, correct orientation, cleanliness | Soft or delicate cell core, tight tolerances | Pick-and-place, fixture loading |
| Electrolyte filling | Cell body and electrolyte | Accurate dosing, dry room, chemical safety | Corrosive or sensitive chemicals, contamination risk | Loading/unloading, sealed handling support |
| Sealing | Filled cell | Leak-tight sealing, cleanliness | Heat/process integration, quality assurance | Handling, positioning, inspection |
| Formation | Sealed cells in trays | High throughput, traceability, safety | Heavy tray flows, long process times, many cells | Tray loading/unloading, AMR transport |
| Aging | Formed cells | Controlled storage, traceability, defect detection | Large inventory, space utilization | AMR/AGV logistics, automated storage |
| Testing / grading | Finished cells | Reliable measurement, classification, data linkage | High volume, EOL integration | Automated test loading, sorting |
| Intralogistics | Rolls, trays, cells, containers | Stable flow, low manual handling, traceability | Dry room constraints, fleet management | AGV/AMR transport, automated buffering |

## Requirement Categories to Refine

- Dry room compatibility
- Particle and contamination control
- Electrostatic discharge protection
- Delicate material handling
- Precision positioning
- Cycle time
- Payload
- Human-robot safety
- MES and traceability integration
- Maintenance and downtime impact
